## Direction contract

THESIS: A meeting-notes tool that shows PROOF it reconstructed truth, not a generic "AI-powered" pitch. The page refuses the default hero-CTA-features-testimonials AI-SaaS template; it opens with the mechanism itself running live.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (#0A0A0C), soft radial color glows (not gradient text) doing the work of accent — status-red and status-green pooling behind proof cards, exactly as the pinned reference comps show. Rounded-2xl surfaces, 1px neutral borders, pill badges with a small solid dot + one colored word (never a gradient fill). Colored progress/underline bars carry status meaning, never decorative borders. Display face: Space Grotesk (self-hosted, geometric, confident — distinct from default Inter-as-display); Inter stays for UI/body; JetBrains Mono stays for evidence/transcript text and numerals, functionally justified (data, not costume). Depth via soft layered shadow + status-colored glow, never a flat colored halo.

STORY: A skeptical PM/team-lead lands, sees a real transcript line become a structured claim in front of them (the AiStreamingText moment), sees the "before/after" of a messy meeting turned into a clean decided/committed/conflict/unknown state, and believes this is mechanically different from a summarizer — then clicks through to the working tool.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned headline making a specific, provable claim (not "AI-powered meeting intelligence"). Beside/below it, a live evidence-to-claim demo card: a transcript line streams in via AiStreamingText, then resolves into a status-pill claim card — the mechanism dramatized, not described. No kicker/eyebrow. No stock hero illustration.

FORM: Brief-pinned by two user-supplied reference comps (dark analytics comparison cards with red/green glow; warm glass-pill mobile app chrome) — direction taken directly from the brief per "the brief wins," concept-seed roll skipped per new-work.md's pinned-direction rule. Seed key: n/a (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
